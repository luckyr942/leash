# 3-minute demo video

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Landing page | "Freelancers do the work, then spend hours writing invoices and awkwardly chasing late payments. Leash fixes both." |
| 0:15–0:50 | New invoice → paste a WhatsApp chat → **Extract invoice** → highlight a field it flagged as missing → fill it | "I paste the client chat. Gemini pulls out the client, the work, $500 and the due date, and tells me what's missing instead of guessing." |

| 2:20–2:40 | Show it moved to Waiting ("next step: final notice on day 22"), then the 40-day invoice suggesting a call | "It won't nag: one reminder per stage, and after 30 days it tells me to call instead of sending another email." |

| 2:55–3:00 | Logo | "Leash. Get paid, without the awkward follow-ups." |

Tips: record at 1440×900 in light mode, use Settings → Load sample data first, and keep the sandbox client account signed in.
